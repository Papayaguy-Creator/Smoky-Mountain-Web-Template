## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Upload to GitHub
1. Create new repository on GitHub
2. Upload the `index.html` file
3. Enable GitHub Pages in Settings

### 2. Customize Your Content
Edit `index.html` and replace:
- `[Your Business Name]` - Your actual business name
- `[Service 1]`, `[Service 2]`, `[Service 3]` - Your services
- `[Price]` - Your pricing
- `[Your Email]` - Your contact email
- `[Your Phone]` - Your phone number
- `[Your Address]` - Your business address

## 🎨 Customization

### Colors
- Primary blue: `#1E3A8A` (search and replace with your color)
- Accent gold: `#F59E0B` (search and replace with your accent)

### Images
Replace the hero background image URL with your own:
```css
background-image: url('your-image-url-here');
```

## 🤖 Chatbot Setup

The chatbot works out of the box with intelligent responses for:
- Service inquiries
- Pricing questions
- Booking assistance
- Prayer requests
- Business information

For AI-powered responses, add your OpenAI API key:
```javascript
const OPENAI_API_KEY = 'your-api-key-here';
```
