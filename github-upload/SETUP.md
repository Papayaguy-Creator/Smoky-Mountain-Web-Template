## 🏔️ Customization Guide

### 📋 Replace These Placeholders

Before going live, find and replace these in `index.html`:

#### 🏢 Business Information
- `[Your Business Name]` → Your actual business name
- `[Your Email]` → Your contact email 
- `[Your Phone]` → Your phone number
- `[Your Address]` → Your business address

#### 🛍️ Services & Pricing
- `[Service 1]` → e.g., "Mountain Tours"
- `[Service 2]` → e.g., "Cabin Rentals" 
- `[Service 3]` → e.g., "Faith Retreats"
- `[Price]` → Your actual pricing

#### 📧 Email Setup
- `[YOUR_EMAIL_HERE]` → Update in JavaScript (2 locations)

### 🎨 Optional Customizations
- Replace hero background image
- Update testimonials with real reviews
- Add your social media links
- Customize colors and fonts
- Add your business logo

### 🚀 Going Live
1. Upload files to GitHub
2. Enable GitHub Pages in Settings
3. Test all functionality
4. Share your new website!

### 🤖 Chatbot
The chatbot works immediately with smart responses for common questions about services, pricing, and bookings.
